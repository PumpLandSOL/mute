how it works

Three moves. No names.

01

Mint

Deposit USDG on Robinhood Chain. Most becomes hard collateral. The algorithmic share buys $MUTE at market and erases it.

02

Mute

Your mUSD becomes a note encrypted only to you. To everyone else the balance is unreadable.

03

Send

Amount, sender and recipient stay hidden. The ledger holds one nullifier and one commitment.
